    Hollybrook

    On Christmas Eve the sounds of Hollybrook begin to vanish. First the kettles, then the clocks, then the carols. Oscar, Henry and Lottie have run off into the dark, the Midnight Bell will not ring, and nobody knows where the sound is going.

    Isabelle has her glasses, four lost chimes to find, and Margot. Margot can still bark. Nobody knows why.
